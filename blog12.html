Blog 12 : Founder Story | Zomato
Date : 14/oct/2026
  1. Slide 1: Introduction — The Success Story * Topic: Deepinder Goyal, Founder of Zomato. * Core Message: He built a massive food-tech giant, but the company did not start with food delivery at all.
  2. Slide 2: The Beginning — Humble Origins * The Original Idea: It started as "Foodiebay," a simple menu-scanning website. * The Problem Solved: It was created to solve a small, everyday inconvenience—helping office colleagues avoid calling restaurants repeatedly just to check paper menus. * Key Insight: There was no grand, long-term vision of building India’s largest food-tech enterprise on day one; it was simply about solving a real problem for the people right around him.
  3. Slide 3: The Journey — From Menus to a Public Company The timeline highlights how Zomato evolved step-by-step: * 2008: Launched "Foodiebay" as a menu-scanning site. * 2010: Rebranded to Zomato and expanded its offerings beyond basic menus. * 2015: Entered the food delivery sector across major Indian cities. * 2021: Reached a major milestone by going public on the Indian stock exchange (IPO).
  4. Slide 4: The Takeaway — Famous Quote * Quote: "You don't need to see the whole mountain before you take the first step." * Meaning: You do not need to have your entire life or business mapped out perfectly to begin. Taking action on a small scale is more important than waiting for a flawless master plan.
  5. Slide 5: Why It Matters — The Bigger Picture * Evolution of Vision: A grand vision rarely appears at the beginning; it reveals itself naturally as you progress along the journey. * Focus on Execution: Zomato succeeded not because it predicted the future of food delivery in 2008, but because it built a useful tool for an immediate problem. * The Rule of Scale: If you focus on solving the problem right in front of you with excellence, growth and scale will follow organically.
  6. Slide 6: Conclusion * Core Message: "Solve the problem in front of you."
