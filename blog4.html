Blog 04: Zepto: Two Friends, One Dream, A Billion Dollar Company
Date : 23/Aug/2026
  1. Slide 1: Introduction & Founders : Two Friends. One Dream. Zero Money. A Billion Dollar Company. Key Points:Founders: Kaivalya Vohra (19) aur Aadit Palicha (19) - Dono IIT Bombay dropouts. Core Idea: Company banane ke sath-sath daily lifestyle aur quick delivery ko better banana.
  2. Slide 2:The First Challenge - No Money Key Points:Initial Struggles: Shuruat me koi capital, office, team ya investors nahi the. Key Obstacles: No funding, no team, no infrastructure, aur logon ke doubts/criticisms. Drive: Kaivalya Vohra ka quote: "We didn't have money, but we had time, energy and a crazy idea."
  3. Slide 3:The Turning Point - Facing the Real World Key Points:Core Realization: Logon ko everyday essentials ki fast delivery chahiye thi. Action Taken: 10-minute grocery delivery platform ka MVP (Minimum Viable Product) banaya aur early customers ke sath test kiya. Execution: Single app ke andar Groceries, Snacks, Beauty, Personal Care aur Home Essentials cover kiye.
  4. Slide 4:The Struggle - Money Crisis & Fundraising Key Points:Money Crisis: High operational costs (10 min delivery expensive thi), frequent cash burn, aur scaling ke liye capital ki jarurat. Fundraising Timeline:Seed Round (2021): $60 Million (Blume Ventures, others) Series A (2022): $200 Million (Lightspeed, Glade Brook, others) Series B (2023): $340 Million (Nexus Venture Partners, others) Series C (2024): $665 Million (Domestic + International Investors)
  5. Slide 5:The Breakthrough - Scaling Fast Key Points:Rapid Scale: 1 city se 100+ cities tak expansion aur 10,000+ delivery workforce. Key Growth Metrics:100+ Cities in India 10M+ Monthly Active Users 10K+ Delivery Partners 500+ SKUs (Groceries, fashion, beauty, electronics & more)
