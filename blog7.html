Introduction & The Founder's Vision · Subject: Highlight Vineeta Singh, Co-founder & CEO of SUGAR Cosmetics. · The Vision: Frame her journey as a mission to build a brand that every Indian woman could feel proud of, creating 'Bold Makeup for the Modern Indian Woman.' · Founder's Background: · Education: B.Tech in Electronics & Communication from IIT Madras. · Early Career: Gained corporate experience at Bain & Company and Flipkart. · The Big Dream: Wanted to build a brand that celebrates Indian women and makes beauty accessible to all.
  2. 2. Company Overview & Milestones · About the Company: SUGAR Cosmetics is an Indian beauty brand founded in 2012 by Vineeta Singh and Kaushik Mukherjee. It is known for high-quality, long-lasting, and inclusive makeup products designed specifically for Indian skin tones, preferences, and lifestyles. · Key Details: Founded in 2012, headquartered in Gurugram, India, operating in the Beauty & Cosmetics industry. · Timeline of Growth: · 2012: Launched with 10 products. · 2015: Expanded product range and offline presence. · 2017: Entered online marketplaces and grew across India. · 2019: Opened first physical stores and expanded product range. · 2021+: Grew into a household name with a strong global presence.
