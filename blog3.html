Blog 03 :Kamlesh Nanasaheb Ghumare
Date : 17/Aug/2026
  1. Slide 1: The Farmers SonHeading 1 The Farmers Son Key Points:Founder: Kamlesh Nanasaheb Ghumare known as Jugaadu Kamlesh. Background Born and raised in Devarpade village near Malegaon Maharashtra. Education Attended NS Deshmukh Vidyalaya and pursued a BCA degree before dropping out in his second year to focus on solving agricultural challenges. Identity: Farmer Innovator and Entrepreneur.
  2. Slide 2: The Problem Key Points:Real Observation: Kamlesh observed his father and local farmers struggling to carry heavy pesticide tanks on their backs. Physical Strain: Carrying heavy spray tanks caused severe back pain physical fatigue and joint damage. Health Risks: Direct exposure to chemical pesticide fumes posed serious health hazards. Time Wastage: Manual spraying took long hours and reduced overall farming productivity.
  3. Slide 3:The Journey of Innovation Key Points First Prototype Created a bicycle based pesticide spraying cart using local scrap materials. Skill Building: Spent around seven years learning welding soldering and mechanical skills to refine the model. Upgraded Innovation: Built the multipurpose agricultural cart named Bharat K2 for versatile field use.
  4. Slide 4: Shark Tank India Key Points National Platform: Pitched on Shark Tank India in 2022 alongside his cousin and business partner Naru. Investment Deal: Peyush Bansal co founder of Lenskart offered 10 Lakh rupees for 40 percent equity plus 20 Lakh rupees as an interest free loan. Beyond Capital: Received valuable mentorship strategic guidance and national media exposure.
  5. Slide 5: KG Agrotech and Bharat K2 Key Points:Venture Focus: KG Agrotech designs affordable practical and innovative agricultural equipment for farmers. Multipurpose Features: Bharat K2 performs pesticide spraying seed sowing and crop transportation. Target Audience: Specifically engineered for small and marginal Indian farmers.
  6. Slide 6: Lessons and LegacyHeading: 6 Lessons and Legacy Key Points:Spot Real Problems: Identify genuine ground level challenges before building solutions. Learn by Doing: Start small with limited resources and learn through practical execution. Keep Improving: Continuously refine and upgrade the product based on feedback. Right Mentorship: Utilize proper guidance and support networks to scale the business. Core Quote: Big dreams do not need big resources just a problem passion and persistence by Jugaadu Kamlesh.
