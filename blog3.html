Blog 03 :Kamlesh Nanasaheb Ghumare
Date : 17/Aug/2026
  1. Slide 1: The Farmer's Son Key Points:Founder: Kamlesh Nanasaheb Ghumare ("Jugaadu Kamlesh"). Background: Devarpade village (Malegaon, Maharashtra) me paida hue aur pale-badhe. Education: NS Deshmukh Vidyalaya se padhai ki; BCA course shuru kiya par 2nd year me kheti ki problems solve karne ke liye chhod diya. Identity: Farmer, Innovator aur Entrepreneur.
  2. Slide 2:The Problem Key Points:Real Observation: Kamlesh ne apne pita aur doosre kisanon ko peeth par bhaari pesticide tanks uthate hue dekha. Physical Strain: Bhaari tank uthane se kisanon ko sharir me severe dard aur thakan hoti thi. Health Risks: Chemical fumes ke directly contact me aane se health issues hote the. Time Wastage: Manual spraying me bohot ziada waqt barbaad hota tha.
  3. Slide 3: The Journey of Innovation Key Points:First Prototype: Scrap materials aur cycle components ka use karke pehla pesticide spraying cart banaya. Skill Building: Design ko improve karne ke liye 7 saal tak welding, soldering aur mechanical skills khud sikhi. Upgraded Innovation: Advanced "Bharat K-2" cart banaya jo multi-purpose agricultural usage ke liye ready tha.
  4. Slide 4: Shark Tank India (2022) Key Points:National Platform: 2022 me apne cousin aur business partner Naru ke sath Shark Tank India me pitch kiya. The Investment Deal: Peyush Bansal (Co-founder, Lenskart) ne ₹10 Lakh (40% equity ke liye) + ₹20 Lakh (interest-free loan) ka offer diya. Beyond Capital: Funding ke sath-sath strategic mentorship, national exposure aur guidance mila.
  5. Slide 5: KG Agrotech & Bharat K-2 Key Points:Venture Focus: KG Agrotech ka maqsad kisanon ke liye affordable farming equipment banana hai. Multi-Purpose Features: Bharat K-2 cart spraying, seed sowing aur transport – teeno me kaam aata hai. Target Audience: Specially chote aur marginal farmers ki jeb ke hisab se design kiya gaya hai.
  6. Slide 6: Lessons & Legacy Key Points:Spot Real Problems: Hamesha ground level par asal dikkat ko pehchano. Learn by Doing: Bina bade setup ke kaam shuru karo aur karke sikho. Keep Improving: Feedback aur zarurat ke hisab se product ko lagatar upgrade karo. Right Mentorship: Sahi guidance aur support lekar business ko scale karo. Core Message: "Big dreams don't need big resources, just a problem, passion and persistence." – Jugaadu Kamlesh
