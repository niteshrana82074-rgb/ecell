Blog 08 : Founder Success Story | Bhavish Aggarwal
Date : 19/Sep/2026
  1. Slide 1 (01/06): FOUNDER SUCCESS STORY · Subject: Bhavish Aggarwal — Founder, Ola · Tagline: A bad road trip turned into one of India's biggest startups.
  2. Slide 2 (02/06): The Beginning · Subheading: An engineer with a frustrating cab ride. · Content: Bhavish Aggarwal, an IIT Bombay graduate working at Microsoft Research, booked a cab for a road trip to Bandipur. The car broke down, the driver overcharged him, and the experience was frustrating enough to plant an idea: India's cab-booking experience needed to be rebuilt from scratch.
  3. Slide 3 (03/06): The Journey · Subheading: From a bad cab ride to a mobility giant · Timeline: · 2010: Quits Microsoft, founds OlaCabs with Ankit Bhati · 2011: Launches the Ola app in Mumbai · 2014–15: Scales across Indian cities, raises major funding · 2017+: Expands into Ola Electric — EV manufacturing
  4. Slide 4 (04/06): The Takeaway · Quote: “The best startup ideas often come from your own everyday frustration.”
  5. Slide 5 (05/06): Why It Matters · Key Point: He didn't wait for the perfect idea to arrive. · Explanation: Bhavish had a stable, prestigious job at Microsoft Research. He left it not because he had a perfect business plan, but because he'd lived a problem personally and couldn't ignore it. That's often where the strongest startups begin.
  6. Slide 6 (06/06): Conclusion & Call to Action · Message: Your daily frustration could be your next big idea. · Hashtags: #FounderStories #Entrepreneurship #ECellIITB #StartupIndia
