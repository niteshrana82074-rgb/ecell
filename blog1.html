Blog 1 : RedTape : where Style Meets Comfort!
Date : 05/Aug/2026
  1. Slide 01: The Vision. Key Points: 1. Roots: Founded in 1979 under Mirza Tanners. 2. Goal: Started as a local leather business with a global dream to craft world-class footwear.
  2. Slide 02: The Big Move. Key Points: 1. London Flagship: In 1994, RedTape made a bold move and opened its first store in London, UK. 2. Strategic Intent: A deliberate step to showcase fine Indian craftsmanship with an international flair.
  3. Slide 03: Global Positioning & The Reality Key Points:Why London?: 1. To prove international quality, establish a global footprint, and build a premium lifestyle image. Perception vs. Reality: 2. Due to its London location and European aesthetic, many consumers assumed RedTape was a British brand, though it remains 100% Indian at heart
  4. Slide 04: The Launch (1996) Key Points:1. Domestic Entry: RedTape launched in India in 1996, introducing an international design language.2. Audience Fit: Quickly captured young, fashion-conscious customers seeking a mix of modern styling, comfort, and premium build.
  5. Slide 05: The Expansion (2000s Onwards) Key Points:1. Product Diversification: Expanded beyond formal shoes into casual sneakers, apparel (t-shirts), and accessories (wallets, belts, caps). 2. Lifestyle Transformation: Evolved into a complete fashion and lifestyle brand catering to diverse everyday needs.
